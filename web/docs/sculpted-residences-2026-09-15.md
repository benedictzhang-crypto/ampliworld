# Five sculpted CBD residences

The owner's September 15 references define the architectural direction: continuous
sculpted volumes, deep usable terraces, warm timber soffits, recessed full-height
glazing, generous ceiling heights and interiors that read as occupied rooms.
Future building work should follow these spatial principles instead of adding
decorative strips to flat envelopes. Reference photographs are not facade textures.

This slice places five separately numbered 6–8-storey buildings in the eastern CBD
pocket, anchored at world metres (535, -250). Floor height is 4.2m. Each footprint
has a different phase/proportion. The ground lobby has actual walls, glass and an
open entry, furnished lounge and concierge desk. Upper apartments are visual shells;
functional upper-floor access, duplex stairs, bedrooms and full fit-out remain.

All five are original Blender geometry, with balcony slabs, curved glass railings,
timber soffits, terrace furniture, planted pots and courtyard paths. A westward
pedestrian connection meets the existing CBD sidewalk. Placement inspection found
no registered civic/infrastructure collision or housing instance in this pocket.

No new population, ownership or mortgages are fabricated by this visual asset pass.
Photoreal material/lighting quality and detailed landscaping remain below the owner's
reference standard; the Blender render is a review artifact, not a claim of completion.

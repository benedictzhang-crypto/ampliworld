# Neighborhood commerce and hospitality

Original synthetic occupation sampling now uses unequal weights, with permanent identities, ages, accounts and family records retained. These are scenario weights, not a calibrated national or city census. Employer staffing further shapes the CBD job mix. Salary variation is deterministic within a role.

108 existing named mall shops each receive 2–6 residents, including a manager. The sushi restaurant and three nearby new restaurants have operating owners and employees. The auto center has management, sales and technicians. Hospital, school and police service points have assigned staff and work-hour records; this is not a complete physical hospital or policing simulation.

Customer payments transfer to operating accounts; commercial wages debit those accounts. Opening working capital is explicitly transferred from treasury, not minted. Insufficient operating funds accumulate unpaid wage counters. Government-service wages remain treasury-funded. This prototype does not yet include inventory, supplier costs, staffing queues or full company balance sheets. Restaurant ownership is an operating-role record, not newly granted financial wealth.

Hunger decay was adjusted from roughly 1.3 to roughly 2–3 meal requirements/day. Available groceries no longer force every meal to be cooked at home. Restaurant choice considers cash reserves, persona frugality, distance and opening hours. Mall shopping, auto-service and hotel short-stay events now have actual payments and per-business daily visit/revenue counters. These are scenario policies, not evidence of predictive validity.

Original 3D additions: three restaurants near the sushi garden; 70m five-star-style and 45m four-star-style hotels near CBD. Real geometry façades, balconies, entrance gaps and lobby furnishings; no photo façades. Hotel stars describe fictional positioning, not accreditation. Hotel guest rooms are not individually enterable. Existing cross-district transit remains an explicitly abstract timed journey.

Next: calibrate industry/occupation/age/income joint distributions to a chosen real reference city; match training/credentials to every role; reconcile old neighbor/coworker edges after employment changes; merchant capacity and inventory; shift attendance; road-connected pedestrian service catchments; hotel bookings/room occupancy; sustainable payroll and supplier accounting.

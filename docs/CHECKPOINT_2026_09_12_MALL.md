# Courtyard mall checkpoint

Six-storey original `GC-MALL-001` / Aurea Galleria / 金庭汇 added to the metre-space district at local (0, -124), independently of the four existing residences. Walkable district ground now covers 220 × 360 m; this is not a completed 2 × 2 km city.

- Offline merged GLB asset: rounded floor ribbons, bronze fins, four real ground portals, open 36 × 28 m courtyard, planters, seating, basin and sculpture.
- Twenty recessed glass display windows with original bags, shoes, coats, necklaces, watches and diamond rings; six fictional shop labels. Products are geometric objects, not photograph facades.
- Compound collision leaves entrances and courtyard paths open; shop windows are blocked. Mall and residence IDs remain separate.
- All new geometry keeps full detail throughout this block. Existing day/night, locomotion and legacy game route retained.
- Upper floor interiors, stairs/lifts, shop purchasing and inventory integration are explicitly deferred. This is a retail exterior and walkable garden prototype, not a fully functioning shopping economy.

Sources: in-house original geometry. Three.js bundled Helvetiker font used for geometric lettering under its bundled licence. No real luxury logos or copied commercial product meshes.

# Enlarged mall campus

New asset GC-MALL-002 replaces the small mall placement, keeping GC-MALL-001 as an archived asset. Main footprint 225 × 180 m versus previous 90 × 72 m = 6.25× bounding footprint, not six times the height. Six storeys remain at about 31 m. The ring is rebuilt with additional human-scale windows, not a scaled scene object.

- Two real hollow ground-floor galleries open from doors on either side of the central south passage; courtyard and street remain continuous.
- 52 exterior merchandise windows; 48 outdoor parking bays, 16 original parked cars, landscaping, lights and drop-off connection.
- Terrain cutout and analytic descending ramp from +0.17 m to −4.2 m. B1 entry vestibule is accessible; its rear door stays closed pending full garage construction. No complete basement garage is claimed.
- Extended ground envelope 430 × 620 m; existing residences kept in place. Upper retail floors, lifts, shopping and vehicle driving remain pending.

Design references, not replicas or imported imagery: Shenzhen MixC Phase III landscape by Hope Design (https://hope.design/cn/projects/7898/) for open retail frontage, landscape and drop-off; WF Central by KPF (https://www.kpf.com/project/wf-central) for stone/metal vitrines, retail arcades and articulated massing. Geometry and product models are original; bundled Helvetiker notice is included.

# Aurea Galleria — Chinese luxury mall reference and tenant roadmap

Research checked 7 October 2026. This is our own proposed leasing and spatial plan, not a reproduction of another mall, an affiliation claim, or a complete current tenant directory. Brand references guide differentiated concept interiors; they do not establish licensed assets or commercial partnerships.

## What the official references actually establish

- [Beijing SKP services](https://www.skp-beijing.com/service/): B1 supermarket, 2F alterations and watch repair, 3F beauty/SPA, 4F RENDEZ-VOUS, 5F membership services and concierge coverage. Adopt the service ecosystem, not just luxury signs.
- [WF Central opening tenant release](https://www.wfcentral.cn/en/uploadfile/2017/1222/20171222023306507.pdf): a **2017 historical** official list mixes luxury, fashion, lifestyle/wellness and dining; includes Bottega Veneta, Chaumet, Gucci, Loewe, Prada, Moleskine and yoga. Not verified as today's tenant list; the dynamic current directory was not reliably retrievable. Do not invent current floor numbers.
- [Saks NYC flagship](https://www.saksfifthavenue.com/locations/newyork/): Beauty on 2, a dedicated shoe experience, restaurants/cafés, styling, alterations, eyewear and beauty services. Transfer destination departments and services into a Chinese-style enclosed mall with atria, linked floors and food-led upper levels.

## Functional completeness beyond retail

Backlog: concierge/information on every public level; alterations; watch and leather repair; gift wrap; parcel lockers; stroller/wheelchair loans; nursing/family rooms; accessible toilets; lost property; first aid/AED; prayer/quiet room; VIP shopping suites; beauty treatment rooms; customer complaints/returns; ATM; grocery cold chain; loading/receiving; waste sorting; cleaner closets; staff dining/lockers; security/control room; fire refuge/egress; HVAC plant and kitchen extraction; EV charging and parking assistance. Existing toilets, lifts, escalators, freight circulation and garage should be audited before adding duplicates. Visible vents do not imply an engineered HVAC simulation.

## Implemented increment and limits

Follow-up: nine additional physical concept rooms now occupy L3 east and both L4 wings: auto showroom (four original concept vehicles, configuration kiosks and material samples), shoe salon, alterations atelier, watch repair, concierge/gift wrapping, members lounge, styling suite, beauty/SPA and art gallery. The tenant manifest now has 26 rooms. Original abstract artwork is procedural, not copied. The mall directory links to the existing independent `GC-AUTO-001` campus; workshop work remains there, not upstairs in the mall. New rooms have no booking, repair execution or financial transaction workflow yet. Proposed service needs above remain a checklist; these nine are modeled, not operationally completed.

The new `mall-tenants-plan.json` and `GC-MALL-TENANTS-001` manifest define **17 physical concept stores**: McDonald's, Starbucks, Richard Mille, bookshop/café, Chanel tailoring, Sephora, Loewe, Balenciaga, Dior, Hermès, Apple, Piglet gifts, hi-fi, furniture, healthy meals, Moleskine and chocolate. Each has dimensions, level, entry, interior fixtures and colliders. Existing boutiques and sports anchors remain. The floor directory exposes only actual visit destinations, not this proposed brand backlog.

Also implemented: stone finish over existing cut-out floor plates, ceiling vent/speaker fixtures, roof garden seating/guards and sports store detail. Audio playback is not connected. Transactions, inventory, staffing and resident demand are not yet connected to these new rooms. Their geometry is a concept fit-out, not a photoreal, fully bespoke finished flagship.

Next acceptance gate: allocate each missing tenant a unique unit/level/polygon; reconcile duplicates and circulation; design category-specific façades/products; add service spaces and back-of-house; then connect staffing, budgets, inventory and observable customer visits. A name in this document is never evidence of a completed store.
